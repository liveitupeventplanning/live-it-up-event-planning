How it started

01

It started with simply
loving to plan.

Before Live It Up was a business, event planning was something I naturally gravitated toward. I loved planning for friends and family, and I was always happy to volunteer when there was a corporate event to organize at work.

There was something about taking all the little pieces — the people, timing, vendors, details and ideas — and turning them into one experience that just clicked for me.

Eventually, I realized I wanted to do more of it. Live It Up became a way to bring that same care, organization and excitement to other people's important moments.

Meet your planner

Hi, I'm Mya.

I'm the founder and event planner behind Live It Up.

For me, event planning has always been more than putting together a schedule or choosing the right décor. I love taking an idea and turning it into an experience that people are genuinely excited to be part of.

Whether it's a birthday, wedding, corporate event, fundraiser or something completely unique, my goal is to understand what you envision, take the stress out of bringing it together, and create an event that feels like you.

“You should be able to enjoy your event — not spend it worrying about the details.”
