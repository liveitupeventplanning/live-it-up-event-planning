BEHIND THE PLANNING

Meet Mya.

Mya Allison · Founder & Event Planner

For me, event planning has always been more than putting together a schedule or choosing the right décor. I love taking an idea and turning it into an experience that people are genuinely excited to be part of.

Long before starting Live It Up Event Planning, I was the person who loved planning events for friends and family and volunteering to help organize corporate events at work. From working through the little details to seeing everything come together on event day, I found myself continually drawn to the planning process.

That passion eventually became Live It Up Event Planning, a way for me to bring the same care, organization and excitement I put into events for the people around me to clients celebrating their own important moments.

Whether it is a birthday, wedding, corporate event, fundraiser or something completely unique, my goal is simple: understand what you envision, take the stress out of bringing it together, and create an event that feels like you.

Because you should be able to enjoy your event—not spend it worrying about the details.
