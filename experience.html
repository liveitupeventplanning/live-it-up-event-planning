What it reinforced

Good events feel easy
because the planning isn't.

Large events are made up of hundreds of small decisions: who needs what information, when vendors arrive, where guests go, who is covering each task and what happens when plans change.

That experience reinforced the approach behind Live It Up today: plan clearly, communicate early, pay attention to the details and create enough structure that clients can actually enjoy the event they're hosting.
